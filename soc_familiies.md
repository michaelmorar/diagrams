# Sociological Perspectives on Families

## Functionalism
- **Key Sociologists**:
  - **George Peter Murdock**: Identified four functions of the family (sexual, reproductive, economic, educational).
  - **Talcott Parsons**: Focused on primary socialization and the stabilization of adult personalities (Warm Bath Theory).
- **Key Concepts**:
  - Nuclear family as a universal institution.
  - Family functions for the smooth running of society.
  - Gender role socialization.

## Marxism
- **Key Sociologists**:
  - **Friedrich Engels**: Family maintains lines of inheritance.
  - **Eli Zaretsky**: Family as a unit of consumption; absorbs stress from work.
- **Key Concepts**:
  - Family supports capitalism.
  - Reproduction of labor force.
  - Economic exploitation within the family.

## Feminism
- **Key Sociologists**:
  - **Ann Oakley**: Critiqued the conventional family; highlighted gender inequalities.
  - **Delphy and Leonard**: Focused on the exploitation of women within the family.
  - **Fran Ansley**: Women as "takers of shit".
- **Key Concepts**:
  - Patriarchy and gender roles.
  - Dual burden and triple shift.
  - Family as a site of female oppression.

## Interactionism
- **Key Concepts**:
  - Focus on daily interactions and meanings.
  - Family roles and relationships are negotiated.

## New Right
- **Key Concepts**:
  - Emphasis on traditional family values.
  - Criticism of family diversity and welfare dependency.

# Key Studies and Theories

## Family Forms
- **Rapoport and Rapoport**: Identified five types of family diversity (organizational, cultural, class, life course, cohort).

## Conjugal Roles
- **Willmott and Young**: Symmetrical family; joint conjugal roles.
- **Stephen Edgell**: Decision-making in families.
- **Jan Pahl**: Financial control and independence in families.

## Changing Relationships
- **Willmott and Young**: Stratified diffusion; changes in family roles over time.

## Criticisms of Families
- **Zaretsky**: Family as a safe haven for workers.
- **Delphy and Leonard**: Women's unpaid labor benefits men.

## Divorce
- **Giddens**: Romantic love and pure relationships.
- **Hart**: Crisis of capitalism and dual burden.
